About this list

Roles are merged across every tracked portfolio and deduplicated on company, title, and city. Where a company publishes to its own applicant tracking system, that live listing is preferred over the investor's board.

Fresh opportunities, automatically

Portfolio and company job boards are checked twice daily, so new openings appear automatically.

Enjoying Find Jobs?

* This site stores a random ID in your browser to remember which roles you've opened and the views you've saved, plus your first name if you choose to give one. After you choose a résumé, checking a role sends its extracted text to TypeSafe AI for matching. Find Jobs keeps scores in a private cache and temporarily uses a hashed network address to limit requests; it does not save résumé text. Clearing site data removes your browser history and saved views. Cached assessments expire after one day.
