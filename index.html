About this list

Roles are merged across every tracked portfolio and deduplicated on company, title, and city. Where a company publishes to its own applicant tracking system, that live listing is preferred over the investor's board.

Fresh opportunities, automatically

Portfolio and company job boards are checked twice daily, so new openings appear automatically.

Browsing preferences are saved in this browser. Optional Google sign-in saves your account, resumes, saved jobs, and analysis results on our server. Resume analysis sends extracted resume text and the job description to TypeSafe AI. We keep each resume's original file and the text read from it. Removing a resume hides it from your account and erases its file after 30 days, but its text and match results stay on our server; clearing browser data does not delete server records either.
